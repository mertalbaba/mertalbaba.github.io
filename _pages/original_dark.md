<h2 id="bio">bio</h2>

I am a PhD student at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/) and the [Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems](https://ps.is.mpg.de/), co-advised by [Michael J. Black](https://ps.is.mpg.de/person/black) and [Andreas Krause](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/people/krausea/). I am also an [ELLIS PhD student](https://ellis.eu/phd-postdoc) and a [CLS Fellow](https://learning-systems.org/phds).

My research focuses on **learning from humans at scale** and **self-improvement in Robotics and Reinforcement Learning**. I study how robots can learn from human demonstrations, motion, and video, and improve their skills through interaction.

I am currently interning at **Meta in London**, working on **world action models**. From May to September, I interned at [Vesoma](https://vesoma.com/) with **Martin Riedmiller**, where I developed **VioLA** and **Dr. Octopus: Universal Hand Controller**. Before that, I was a Machine Learning Research Intern at **Meshcapade** (July–December 2025), developing GURMO for embodiment-agnostic robot motion generation.

Previously, I spent a year at USC, earned my MSc in Mechanical Engineering at Bilkent University under [Yildiray Yildiz](https://yildirayyildiz.com/about/), and completed my BSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Bilkent.

<h2 class="original-section-heading" id="research">research</h2>

I work on learning control policies that can transfer the breadth of human behavior to robots. My research combines **imitation learning**, **reinforcement learning**, and **generative models**, with an emphasis on learning from diverse human data at scale and improving policies through experience. My work spans humanoid control, dexterous manipulation, and goal-conditioned learning.

Recent projects include **VioLA**, a generalist humanoid control policy learned from human and robot demonstrations; **Dr. Octopus: Universal Hand Controller**, an ongoing project; and **NIL**, which learns robot skills from generated videos. I also work on policy improvement at test time (**GC-TTT**) and adaptive reward learning for imitation (**RILe**).

**NIL was published at CVPR 2026**, and I gave a spotlight talk on it at the **2nd Workshop on Agents in Interaction, from Humans to Robots**. **GC-TTT was published at ICML 2026**.
