<section class="section shell research-interests" id="research" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <h2 id="research-title">Research interests</h2>
  <p>My research focuses on robot learning and reinforcement learning at scale, particularly <strong>learning from human data and self-improvement</strong>. I am interested in using human motion, video, demonstrations, and synthetic data to give robots a broad foundation of skills, then enabling them to refine and extend those skills through their own experience. The aim is generalist policies that keep improving and adapt to new tasks and environments.</p>
</section>
