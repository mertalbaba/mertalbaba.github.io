Mert Albaba

PhD Student in Robotics and Reinforcement Learning

I am a PhD student at ETH Zürich and the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, co-advised by Michael J. Black and Andreas Krause. I am also an ELLIS PhD student and a CLS Fellow.

I am currently interning at Meta in London, Reality Labs, working on world action models (WAMs) and
self-supervised learning.

Previously, I interned at Vesoma with Martin Riedmiller, developing generalist humanoid control policies, and at Meshcapade, working on text-to-motion synthesis for humanoid robots.

Research interests

My research focuses on robot learning and reinforcement learning at scale, particularly learning from human data and self-improvement. I am interested in using human motion, video, demonstrations, and synthetic data to give robots a broad foundation of skills, then enabling them to refine and extend those skills through their own experience. The aim is generalist policies that keep improving and adapt to new tasks and environments.
